## Folio PDF Studio 2.5.1

- Bookmark filtering separates query, page/matching conditions and result actions. Disclosure arrows share an 8 px label gap, with consistent focus feedback and improved small-window accessibility.
- Move artwork and resize its corners on the PDF reader surface, with aspect-ratio locking, rotated/mirrored/sheared geometry and Esc cancellation. Each applied gesture is undoable.
- Increase studio reference backgrounds from 1600 to the embedded engines' 2048-pixel limit. Direct placement uses the PDF reader's detailed page rendering.
- Word and Excel exports can skip OCR review for this export, or skip OCR entirely. Recognized text may contain errors; unapplied review data in the original document is preserved.
- Keep document-tool navigation and actions reachable in short windows. Align text and icons, use consistent outlined disclosure arrows and distinguish document actions with dedicated icons.
- Offer explicit draft discard before closing, report close failures and provide a wait/force-exit choice after a stalled close request.
